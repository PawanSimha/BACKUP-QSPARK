Before you switch

Offline is not a maintenance lock. Customers will see a paused-booking notice on the booking page, and the API refuses new bookings until the system is online again.

Recovery stays possible. Super Admins keep full access to this page while offline, so the switch can always be reversed. Do not block your own account at the same time.

Every change is audited. The transition, actor and previous value are written to audit_logs.

If you are going offline for a planned closure, announce it to floor staff first so walk-in customers are redirected.
